Platform

One platform. Everyone's console.

Supervisors run the floor live, agents work their queues from a desk with a phone in it, administrators provision everything from the browser — and it all runs on infrastructure you control.

For supervisorsFor agentsFor administratorsUnder the hood

For supervisors

Run the floor.

The floor & TV-mode wallboard, live
Every queue and every agent on one screen — waiting callers, active calls, who is paused and why — with an 8-tile real-time KPI strip (SLA, ASA, abandon rate, longest wait), threshold breach alerts, and a dedicated TV wallboard mode.
Listen, whisper, barge & QA scorecards
Hear an agent’s call silently, coach them without the caller hearing, or join the conversation — from your own phone, with every session logged. Evaluate completed calls against configurable QA scorecards and sampling rules.
Workforce management (WFM)
Plan shifts and weekly rosters, track real-time schedule adherence, approve agent leave requests, and view half-hourly call-volume and staffing forecasts.
Assign, control & approve
Hand a waiting caller to a free agent, run warm or blind transfers, escalate, move a call to another queue, or review dual-control Maker-Checker approval requests from the floor.
Teams & language skills
Group queues into teams, tag queues and agents with language skills, and scope a supervisor to the teams they lead — their floor, streams, and reports show only those queues.
Outbound campaigns & NPS analytics
Launch outbound auto-dialer campaigns and track post-call IVR and dynamic SMS Net Promoter Score (NPS) results per agent and queue.
AI-summarized queue voicemail & reports
Read on-cluster AI transcripts and English summaries with urgency tags, call the customer back with one click, and run timezone-aware agent, queue, and company reports with in-browser recording playback.

For agents

Work the queue, not the tooling.

A phone in the browser — with keyboard control & warm transfer
The desk registers as your line and takes calls directly — answer, mute, hold, keypad, blind transfer, or warm consultation transfer — with full Alt+Shift keyboard shortcuts so you never leave the keyboard.
5-tab Customer CRM workspace
Caller context opens automatically beside the call: schema-driven customer profile, unified interaction journey, Positive ID & Step-Up OTP verification, and audited service actions with Maker-Checker support.
Omnichannel inbox
Handle web chat, AI bot handoffs, WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram conversations from the same stage as voice queues.
One-click presence & queue choice
Go available with one click, pause with a reason — break, lunch, meeting, training — and join or leave individual queues as coverage shifts through the day.
Wrap-up notes & multiple dispositions
Every call ends in a structured wrap-up with a note and one or more dispositions — resolved, follow-up, escalate — kept with the caller’s history for the next agent.
Voicemail inbox with on-cluster AI summaries
Play messages in the browser, read the Whisper transcript and Gemma English summary with urgency and extracted callback number, and dial back with one click.
Personalized workspace & Mobile PWA
Choose your UI palette, dark/light scheme, and four layout presets synced to your account, export personal analytics to CSV, or install the desk as a Mobile PWA.

For administrators

Provision everything from the browser.

Tenants
Separate companies with their own users, extensions, queues, teams, and numbers — each with its own time zone and per-tenant voicemail, recording, and call-monitoring toggles.
Users, roles, Passkeys & Enterprise SSO
Five roles from agent and supervisor to platform operator, with WebAuthn/FIDO2 passkeys, TOTP 2FA, and Enterprise SSO (OIDC, SAML 2.0, LDAP). Extensions as the universal dial target; devices with per-line SIP credentials and caller ID.
Numbers & carrier trunks
Inbound numbers mapped to extensions; upstream trunks with IP- or password-based auth, provisioned end to end.
Queues, tuned
Service-level targets, queue length caps, comfort messages on an interval, per-queue music on hold, press-zero escape to voicemail — and eight ring strategies:
ring allleast recentfewest callsrandomround-robin memoryround-robin orderedlinearweighted random
Auto attendants, IVR self-service & conferences
Voice prompts with keypad menus, API-driven multi-node IVR self-service flows, post-call IVR NPS surveys, and PIN-protected conference rooms with separate leader and participant codes.
Prompts, retention & SIEM audit stream
Upload announcements and music on hold with automatic Rust-powered audio conversion; configure automated retention windows for recordings and logs; stream CEF audit events to your SIEM.

…and the plumbing you'd hope for.

An API for everything

The console runs entirely on the platform’s REST API — everything it does, your tooling can do too. OpenAPI documentation included.

Live by default

System, call-center, queue, and agent state stream to the browser over Server-Sent Events — no refresh button.

Provisioning without reloads

Configuration is written to the PBX’s realtime database. New tenants, extensions, and queues go live without touching a config file.

Logs for every angle

Call detail records plus per-call, per-queue, per-agent, and per-endpoint event logs — the raw material for any question.

A configuration audit trail

Every configuration change is versioned, so you can see what changed and when.

Half-hourly queue statistics

Queue performance is aggregated every 30 minutes for interval and service-level reporting.

Media storage you control

Recordings, prompts, and music on hold live in S3-compatible object storage on your infrastructure.

Roles that map to reality

Five roles — agent, supervisor, company admin, and two operator tiers — scoped per tenant, with JWT sessions and silent refresh.

Two front ends, one API

An admin console for provisioning and reports, and a separate agent desk — one always-open screen with the phone, the queue, the omnichannel inbox, and the 5-tab customer CRM workspace in it.

A browser phone that stays behind the edge

The desk’s phone is an ordinary SIP endpoint: its signaling and media pass through the same SIP edge and media anchor as every desk phone. The PBX opens nothing to serve it.

Lab-tested to 1,000 concurrent calls

Kernel-space RTP forwarding (xt_RTPENGINE) and a dedicated stateless call-router tier deliver 1,000 concurrent calls with two-way media and zero-drop routing across API rollouts.

On-cluster voicemail AI

Optional Whisper transcription and Gemma English summaries extract urgency and callback numbers from voicemails entirely on your own cluster — zero third-party AI cloud.
