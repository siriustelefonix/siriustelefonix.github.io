Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-10

The short version

This site exists so you can learn about Sirius Telefonix and request a demo. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from third-party servers. The only personal data we receive is what you type into the demo form — and we use it for exactly one thing: arranging your demo.

What we collect

If you submit the demo-request form: your name, work email, company name, and any optional details you choose to share (company size, role, current phone system, your message). If you email us instead, we receive whatever you send.

How it's used

To reply to you, schedule the walkthrough, and follow up on that conversation. We don't add you to marketing lists, send newsletters, or share or sell your details to anyone for their own purposes.

Where it goes

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by a form-processing service acting on our behalf; the conversation then continues over ordinary email. Hosting infrastructure may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability.

How long we keep it

For as long as the conversation is live, and no longer than needed afterwards. Ask us to delete your details at any time and we will.
